Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Short version: this site collects only what you type into the contact form — name, email, and your project brief — and it's used for one thing: replying to you. Nothing is sold, tracked, or shared beyond what's needed to get that reply to your inbox.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to this portfolio website and the contact form on it. It's written to be plain and short rather than exhaustive — if anything here is unclear, use the contact details at the bottom to ask.

What information is collected

The only personal information collected is what you voluntarily submit through the contact form:

  • Name — so I know who I'm replying to
  • Email address — so I can reply
  • Project type and budget range — the options you select
  • Project brief — whatever details you choose to share about the work

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and does not collect any information automatically just from you browsing the pages.

How it's used

Form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if we move forward, to scope and deliver the project you're asking about. Your information is never used for marketing, never sold, and never shared with advertisers.

Where it goes

The contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form-delivery service that routes your submission into my inbox. Formspree briefly stores submissions to do this — see Formspree's own privacy policy for how they handle that. No other third party has access to what you submit.

How long it's kept

Submissions are kept only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and, where a project goes ahead, for the duration of that working relationship plus normal record-keeping. You can ask for your information to be deleted at any time — see below.

Your rights

Whether you're reaching out from the US, the UK, or elsewhere, you can ask at any time to:

  • See what information is held about you
  • Have it corrected if it's wrong
  • Have it deleted

UK and EU visitors have these rights under GDPR specifically; they're extended here to every visitor regardless of location.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top will change with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
